## What we keep

**Your email address**, if you join the waitlist, and with it the page you joined from and the time.

**Your answers**, if you fill in the questions after joining. None of them is required.

Nothing else. The site has no accounts, no comments and no forms besides these.

## What it is used for

To send you the monthly update about the build, and now and then a question about what the hardware should have. The answers decide what gets built first.

Your address is not sold, rented or given to anyone, and it is not used for advertising.

## Where it is held

The list is held by **Sender** (sender.net), the service that sends the update. The form on this site passes your address to Sender through a small relay run on **Cloudflare**, which keeps no copy of it.

## Counting visits

The site counts page views with **Cloudflare Web Analytics**. It sets no cookie and stores nothing in your browser, and it does not follow you from one site to another. It tells us which pages are read and which links brought people here.

That is why there is no cookie banner: there are no cookies.

## Leaving

Every update has a link that takes you off the list at once. To have your address and answers deleted, or to get a copy of them, reply to the confirmation email or to any update.

## Changes

If this page changes in a way that matters, the list is told in the next update.
